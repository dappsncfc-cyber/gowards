Compassion & Experience

Gowards Independent Funerals offer a complete funeral service to Fakenham and North Norfolk with the assurance of being wholly independent. We have offered many years of experience, sympathy and care to our clients.

This experience enables Adrian and the team to be able to meet any requests you or your family may have. Whether you wish to visit our office in Bridge Street, Fakenham or would like us to visit you in the comfort of your own home, we offer a sympathetic and discrete efficient service.

Available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
